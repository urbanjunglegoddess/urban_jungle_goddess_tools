    The run

    How it fills: sessions run from now to your stop time, back to back with a break between each. Your blocks drop onto them in order — first block, first session. Leftover sessions read open focus; blocks that don't fit get flagged to carry into the next window. The trailing break is dropped by default so you're not resting on the way out the door.

    Holding your list: blocks save in this browser and reload when you come back. Open or host this as a real page and they stick; some in-app previews won't keep them.
